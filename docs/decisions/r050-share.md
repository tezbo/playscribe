# Decision note: Share an update as a card
Date / owner / card: 6 Nov 2026, Ezra (draft for Nadia), r050-share (0.5.0, in qa)

1. **Why.** Nadia's 0.5.0 roadmap item 19: let a player send one update to a friend as a picture, so a squad can see "the patch that fixed it". Art and copy are done (brand/share, handoff/0.5.0/follow-news-similar-share-copy.md).
2. **Still needed?** Partly. Sharing is a way for PS5 players to talk about an update, which fits "keeps players in the know". It is not core to knowing what changed for yourself.
3. **Value and cost.** Value: medium-low guess, no usage data yet. Cost: low, already built and designed; ongoing upkeep is one card layout and its text. Worth keeping because the cost is paid and it carries the brand outside the app.
4. **Alternatives.** (a) System share of plain text only: cheaper, but nothing to look at and no brand. (b) Share a link: needs a public page, which is out (no hosting, and the rules ban source links). (c) Do nothing: saves upkeep, loses a free word-of-mouth surface.
5. **Decision.** Keep, with one change: check after the next TestFlight round whether anyone used it, using tester feedback because we hold no analytics. Scrap if no tester shares in two rounds.
6. **Learning.** Build the share surface only when it also gives us a reason to look at usage; otherwise we ship a feature we cannot judge.
