# Decision note: Magic companion (A now, B pilot on one game)
Date / owner / card: 7 Nov 2026, Claude thread for Terry, card magic-companion (approved by Terry 7 Nov 2026 20:55 AEDT: "Agree with recommendation - do it")

1. **Why.** Terry: the app feels static, clunky and hard to navigate, and is not a companion to keep open beside the PS5.
2. **Still needed?** Yes. The north star is a made-for-Duo app that keeps players in the know; today there is no reason to stay open between updates.
3. **Value and cost.** Worth = Duo identity, first impression and reasons to return, against QA load (5 poses x light/dark) and content upkeep. A is cheap and certain; B is moderate and proven by a pilot. Costs not estimated: Theo sizes with agent-estimate.
4. **Alternatives.** A polish and navigation (smallest); B companion layer for one game; C full Field Guide first (biggest, parked); D do nothing (rejected: planned items do not touch navigation).
5. **Decision.** Do A now: labelled rail, one navigation model across poses, serif headings, per-game accent, haptics and motion with Reduce Motion fallbacks, sound off by default. Design B in parallel and pilot it on Dawnwalker with own-written text only (CC BY-SA credit clashes with the no-credits rule). Hold C. Displaces: intro-tour step 3, hero autoplay, theme packs.
6. **Learning.** When a product feels flat, check the reasons to return and the number of ways to get lost before adding features.
