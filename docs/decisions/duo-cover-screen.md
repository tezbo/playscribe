# Decision note: Duo front cover screen and Duo-only opportunities
Date / owner / card: 7 Nov 2026, Sam's research thread, cards `duo-closed-cover`, `duo-hinge-peek`, `duo-two-games` (all ideas)

1. **Why.** Terry asked (6 Nov) whether the app can show something on the outer display while open (animated game cover or Playscribe cover) and to use everything Duo offers.
2. **Still needed?** Yes: "made for Duo" is the north star. The goal is "the Duo feels alive on both faces", not that exact mechanism.
3. **Facts (checked 7 Nov, Apple sources).** The only API that puts app content on the outer display while the device is open is `CameraCaptureAccessory` (scene accessory, tech talk 111464 and "Registering a camera capture accessory on iPhone Duo"). It needs a visible camera preview and an active capture session; a session started just to host it is unsupported. Apple DTS (forum thread 847993, Sep 2026): it is "the only way to use both inner and outer displays at the same time"; camera cases "are the only supported and intended use cases for dual display at present". So G-12 stands for our app: no app-drawn cover while open. Correction (7 Nov, after Terry's challenge): the accessory content is any SwiftUI view (teleprompter, countdown), so "camera previews only" was too strong; the limit is the active camera session, not the content. Live Activities, widgets and StandBy are system surfaces (StandBy runs on either display per Apple's newsroom); Apple documents no rule for what the outer display shows while the app is open on the inner one, so that is unverified and needs a device test. AlarmKit on the outer display was mentioned in a search snippet only; unverified, not relevant.
4. **Options.** (a) Fake a camera session to host a cover: unsupported, App Review risk, privacy cost. Scrap. (b) Make the Closed pose the front cover: when closed the app IS the outer display, so the hero (Continue game art, latest update chip) becomes a designed cover with slow, Reduce Motion-safe art motion. Cheap, in our layout path. (c) Do nothing. (d) Use unused Duo APIs on the inner display (below).
5. **Decision.** Kept, as (b), with Terry's go on 12 Nov 2026 07:27 UTC after the mock-up (https://claude.ai/artifact/E3BFLUojqyY5dzJEcoMve2): the Closed-pose cover ships with three user-selectable motion modes (Trailer loop, Key-art motion, Still) in Settings; spec `docs/cover-motion.md`. Scrap (a). (d) stays as separate cards. Product (Nadia) and design (Iris) not yet consulted; they weigh in before any card leaves ideas.
   Unused Duo APIs in the app today (grep of `ios/`): `onHingeChange` / `UIHingeInteraction` (live hinge angle in Book pose: page lifts, peek of next update); multiple scenes (`UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` is false; two games' patches side by side in Split View, `UIWindowSceneActivationAction` hides itself on the outer display); `stateRestorationActivity` for scene reconnects.
   Stress test: (b) fails if the art motion fights G-9 or AX5 hero caption rules; best case for (a) is a delightful open-pose cover, but Apple says accessory content must never be essential and the session is unsupported.
6. **Learning.** Terry decides from a working mock-up, not a description: the cover was parked for five days on prose and approved in 12 minutes once he could tap it. Also: check Apple's forums and talks, not just the HIG: DTS answered "is there a cover API?" in one line, and "the closed pose is the cover" was already ours.

### Outcome A: an accessory draws on the outer display while open
- Design: a view-only "Playscribe cover" scene on the outer display: the Continue game's art with slow motion, latest update chip, no controls (nothing to tap; it is `ExternalNonInteractiveAccessory`). It follows the open game, so opening a game book changes the cover.
- Rules: Reduce Motion and Video Autoplay off give a still cover (G-9); it is an enhancement only, never the sole place for any information (Apple: the system can withdraw it); Settings toggle to turn it off; hidden when the system reports it unavailable (`onAvailabilityChange`). No source names or credits (CLAUDE.md). Art is the existing cover art, no new data.
- Update G-12: replace "no outer-cover content while open" with the verified behaviour and the iOS version it needs.
- Risks to test on a real Duo: the role may target a connected external screen only; thermal and battery cost of animating a second display; App Review treatment. Card: `duo-open-cover` (new), after Nadia and Iris weigh in.

### Outcome B: neither draws (fallback)
- G-12 stays as written. Scrap the open-pose cover and log the learning.
- Ship the Closed-pose front cover instead (card `duo-closed-cover`), plus the hinge and two-games cards. Optional later: a Live Activity or StandBy widget for "update just landed" (system surfaces; needs its own decision note).
- Learning: the SDK knew more than the docs; grep the swiftinterface before saying "no API", and probe before promising either way.
