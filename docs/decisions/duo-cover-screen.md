# Decision note: Duo front cover screen and Duo-only opportunities
Date / owner / card: 7 Nov 2026, Sam's research thread, cards `duo-closed-cover`, `duo-hinge-peek`, `duo-two-games` (all ideas)

1. **Why.** Terry asked (6 Nov) whether the app can show something on the outer display while open (animated game cover or Playscribe cover) and to use everything Duo offers.
2. **Still needed?** Yes: "made for Duo" is the north star. The goal is "the Duo feels alive on both faces", not that exact mechanism.
3. **Facts (checked 7 Nov, Apple sources).** The only API that puts app content on the outer display while the device is open is `CameraCaptureAccessory` (scene accessory, tech talk 111464 and "Registering a camera capture accessory on iPhone Duo"). It needs a visible camera preview and an active capture session; a session started just to host it is unsupported. Apple DTS (forum thread 847993, Sep 2026): it is "the only way to use both inner and outer displays at the same time"; camera cases "are the only supported and intended use cases for dual display at present". So G-12 stands: no cover while open. AlarmKit on the outer display was mentioned in a search snippet only; unverified, not relevant.
4. **Options.** (a) Fake a camera session to host a cover: unsupported, App Review risk, privacy cost. Scrap. (b) Make the Closed pose the front cover: when closed the app IS the outer display, so the hero (Continue game art, latest update chip) becomes a designed cover with slow, Reduce Motion-safe art motion. Cheap, in our layout path. (c) Do nothing. (d) Use unused Duo APIs on the inner display (below).
5. **Decision (recommended, awaiting Terry).** Scrap (a). Do (b) first, then (d) as separate cards. Product (Nadia) and design (Iris) not yet consulted; they weigh in before any card leaves ideas.
   Unused Duo APIs in the app today (grep of `ios/`): `onHingeChange` / `UIHingeInteraction` (live hinge angle in Book pose: page lifts, peek of next update); multiple scenes (`UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` is false; two games' patches side by side in Split View, `UIWindowSceneActivationAction` hides itself on the outer display); `stateRestorationActivity` for scene reconnects.
   Stress test: (b) fails if the art motion fights G-9 or AX5 hero caption rules; best case for (a) is a delightful open-pose cover, but Apple says accessory content must never be essential and the session is unsupported.
6. **Learning.** Check Apple's forums and talks, not just the HIG: DTS answered "is there a cover API?" in one line, and "the closed pose is the cover" was already ours.
