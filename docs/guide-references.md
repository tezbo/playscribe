# Game guide references (for the "Full game guide")

Terry's reference set lives on his Mac at `references/` and is not committed: it's a commercial
guide (Prima's official guide to *Ni no Kuni: Wrath of the White Witch*, 408 pages, as both PDF and
EPUB). This note describes its patterns only. I sampled pages across the book rather than reading
all of it, so treat the coverage below as indicative.

## What it is

A hardback-style official strategy guide that reads like part of the game's world: walkthrough,
bestiary ("Creatures", numbered Familiars), and art and interview pages, all in the game's
storybook look.

## Layout patterns

| Pattern | What it looks like | Why it's exciting |
|---|---|---|
| **Chapter thumb tabs** | A coloured tab on the page edge with the chapter number and name, running down the spine ("3 · Chapter 5 – The Magic Steps", "5 · Creatures"). | You can find your place by feel; the book has a geography. |
| **Ornate frames** | Every page sits inside a decorative border in the game's art style; full-bleed art pages have a heavy illustrated frame. | It feels like an artefact from the world, not a manual. |
| **Walkthrough strips** | Numbered steps ("8. Finally, …") beside in-game screenshots with dialogue visible, grouped under illustrated section banners named after places ("Mummy's Tummy"). | You read along while you play; the screenshot shows exactly what you should be seeing. |
| **Tip callouts** | Small labelled boxes ("Tip") set into the walkthrough. | Optional depth without breaking the steps. |
| **Bestiary cards** | Dense, consistent cards per creature: number, name, family badge, portrait, stat table, star ratings, abilities, weaknesses and resistances as coloured element chips. | Collectible and scannable; you can compare at a glance. Numbers invite completion. |
| **Index grids** | Small cards in a grid (number, name, sign, HP, weakness, resists) for the whole roster. | A checklist you want to finish. |
| **Full-page art** | Key locations as full-page paintings; concept-art galleries with captioned panels ("Castle Nevermore – Exterior", "… After Defeating Shadar"). | Pure reward and anticipation; it doubles as a souvenir. |
| **Behind the scenes** | A developer interview spread with portrait, and concept-art pages. | Insider access; the "extras" that made these books special. |

## What this suggests for a made-for-Duo guide

- **Tabletop:** the standing half is the "page art" (a map or the current location's painting);
  the flat half is the walkthrough strip, swipeable step by step, with toggles that mark items and
  enemies on the map above (Terry's idea).
- **Book:** a true spread: walkthrough on one page, the matching bestiary cards or map on the
  facing page, with chapter tabs on the outer edge.
- **Closed:** a quick-reference card: the next step, the nearest collectible, the current boss's
  weaknesses as element chips.
- **Open:** the full spread with art, for browsing between sessions.
- Carry the book's devices across: chapter tabs, numbered steps, tip callouts, collectible numbered
  cards with completion counts, and an "extras" chapter for art and interviews.
