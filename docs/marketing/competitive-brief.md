# Competitive brief: Patchbook.gg (draft v0.1)

Owner: Mara. Facts below come from patchbook.gg's own pages (home, about, games), read 6 Nov 2026. Anything the pages do not
state is marked "not stated"; we do not guess.

## What Patchbook says it is
"Patch notes made easy": it tracks public game updates and organises them into readable summaries, categorised changes,
source links and community reaction tools. Users follow games, vote on changes and discuss patches.

## Positioning gap we can own
Patchbook answers "what changed in this PC patch". We found no app that answers "did it land on my PS5, and what changed" in an
iPhone app. Message: **Know what changed, and whether it reached your PS5.**

Store copy holds to the evidence-based tag: the claim is only as strong as an "On PS5" result (PS5 row live and official).

## What we should not do
- Copy community voting or discussion: it is their strength, needs moderation, and does not serve the north star.
- Compare features we cannot show. No claims about their quality or reach.

## Opportunities
- Store keyword set around PS5, patch notes, update alerts (see aso-plan.md).
- A web page per supported game later (SEO) once the site domain exists and Nadia agrees.

## Threats
- They add console coverage or a mobile app. Watch their pages at each grooming.
