# Light and dark appearance plan (roadmap 1.0)

Status: plan only, no code. Recommendation (Theo): a System/Light/Dark setting that defaults to System. Nadia decides
whether this moves earlier than 1.0.

## Today
- The app is locked dark in two places: `UIUserInterfaceStyle` in `ios/Playscribe/Info.plist` and
  `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` in `PlayscribeApp.swift`.
- Brand tokens in `UI/PlayscribeBrand.swift` are single-appearance constants generated by `brand/tools/gen.py`
  from `brand/tokens.json`. `Color.ink0` to `ink4`, `textPrimary` and the rest are aliases of them.
- About 83 uses of `.primary` and `.secondary` already adapt to the system.
- The Home Screen widget already follows the system (ink on dark, vellum on light).

## Hardcoded colour, to review
- `UI/Theme.swift`: three hex colours (`ok`, `simulated`, `watching`).
- About 30 `.black`/`.white` uses: art scrims in `LibraryView`, the video `PlayerView`, `ShareCard`, `PageTurn` shadow,
  the debug overlay. Scrims over art and the player stay dark in both appearances. The share card is a fixed image.
- 41 `.opacity(...)` sites: each needs a look, because a translucent ink fill on a light ground reads as grey.
- `LaunchBackground.colorset` has no light variant; the app icon has no dark or tinted variants.

## Plan
1. **Tokens.** Add light values for the grounds (vellum), text (ink) and dividers to `tokens.json`; change `gen.py` to emit
   dynamic colours (`UIColor` trait provider). One PR. Iris signs off the ink/vellum pairs.
2. **Switch.** Add Settings, Appearance: System (default), Light, Dark, stored in `PersistedState`. Remove the two dark
   locks and apply the choice with `preferredColorScheme`. One PR, with the audit below.
3. **Audit.** Walk the hardcoded sites above. Keep scrims, player and share card dark; fix the rest.
4. **Contrast.** The amber `accent` is likely below 4.5:1 as text on vellum; use `ember600` or darker for text and links
   in light, keep `accent` for fills with `onAccent`. Check with the brand validator and Increase Contrast.
5. **Assets.** Light launch background; app icon dark and tinted variants (Ezra).
6. **QA.** 5 poses × 2 appearances, plus Increase Contrast, for the screens in `docs/release-0.5.0-gate.md`.

## Size and risk
Medium: about 3 PRs plus a QA round. Risks: hero titles over art, the Duo hinge region contrast in light, the share
card's fixed dark look beside a light app, and the new pairs needing Iris's sign-off.
