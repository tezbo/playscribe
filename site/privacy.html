Privacy

Last updated 6 Nov 2026.

Playscribe is published by CodeConnectors. There is no account and we do not sell your data or use it to track you across other apps.

What leaves your device

If you turn on update alerts, the app sends these to our alert service, which runs on Cloudflare: your device's push notification token, which games you follow, whether the build is a test or live build, and, if you set quiet hours, that window and your time zone. We use them only to send you the alerts you asked for. The token is never published, and our logs hold counts only. Turn alerts off in Settings, or in iOS Settings, and you stop receiving them.

We keep this until you ask us to delete it. Email support@codeconnectors.co and we will remove it.

Everything else (your library, reading position, settings) stays on your device.
