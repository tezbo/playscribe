Privacy

Last updated 11 Nov 2026.

Playscribe is published by CodeConnectors. There is no account and we do not sell your data or use it to track you across other apps.

What leaves your device

If you turn on update alerts, the app sends these to our alert service, which runs on Cloudflare: your device's push notification token, which games you follow, whether the build is a test or live build, and, if you set quiet hours, that window and your time zone. We use them only to send you the alerts you asked for. The token is never published, and our logs hold counts only. Turn alerts off in Settings, or in iOS Settings, and you stop receiving them.

We keep this until you ask us to delete it. Email support@codeconnectors.co and we will remove it.

Your follow list

If you turn on "Help keep my games up to date", Playscribe sends our server the games you follow and a random code it makes for your phone. The code is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone, and it is stored only on this phone, not in iCloud. Once a week while you use the app, it also sends a small "still here" note.

What we do with it

We use the list only to choose which games to check every hour. Our checking deletes its copy of your record before anything is published, and our logs hold counts only. Nothing from your record is ever on the public feed or in the app's data. Our server does not store your IP address; it uses it only to limit how fast one address can sign up.

How long we keep it

Up to 90 days after your last "still here" note. We delete it at once when you turn the setting off, turn alerts off for good, or reset the app.

If you also allow alerts

We also send the code Apple gives your phone for alerts, whether this is a test or live version of the app, your language, your quiet hours if you set them, and the alert level you chose for each game.

What we never send

Your name or email, your Apple ID, your phone's name or model, advertising ids, your location, what you read, tap or search, your Owned or Wishlist marks, photos, contacts, or any feedback you write. TestFlight testers' email addresses stay with Apple and never reach our server.

Everything else (your library, reading position, settings) stays on your device.
