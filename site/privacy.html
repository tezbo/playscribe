Privacy

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

Playscribe is published by CodeConnectors. There is no account and we do not sell your data or use it to track you across other apps.

What leaves your device

Your follow list. If you turn on "Help keep my games up to date", Playscribe sends our server the games you follow and a random code it makes for your device. The code is not linked to you, and it is stored only on this device, not in iCloud. Once a week it also sends a short check-in, so we can remove records from devices that stopped using the app. We use the list only to choose which games to check every hour. Our checking deletes its copy of your record before anything is published, and our logs hold counts only. Nothing from your record is ever on the public feed or in the app's data.

What every connection carries. Like any app that connects to a server, the request also carries your IP address, the app and iOS version, and your language setting. We do not store them. The one exception is an address we block because it is being used to abuse the service. We use an IP address only to limit how fast one address can sign up.

Update alerts. If you turn on update alerts, the app also sends the code Apple gives your device for alerts, the games you follow with the alert level you chose for each, whether this is a test or live version of the app, and your quiet hours with your time zone if you set them. Test builds also send whether you turned on team alerts. We use these only to send you the alerts you asked for. The code is never published, and our logs hold counts only.

How long we keep it

Your follow list: we keep it until you turn the setting off. If you stop using the app, we remove it within 90 days. Deleting the app and installing it again also removes it. If you also have alerts on, the games you follow stay on our server for your alerts, and are deleted when you turn alerts off as well.

Update alerts: kept while alerts are on. We delete them when you turn alerts off in iOS Settings (the next time you open the app), or when you delete the app and install it again. If you delete the app without turning alerts off first, they are removed the next time an alert to your device fails (we keep only your games if you are on the follow list), or sooner if you email us.

When your device stops receiving alerts. If Apple tells us your device can no longer receive alerts, which usually means the app was deleted, we clean up. A record that only had alerts is deleted whole: the alert code, the random code, your alert settings and your games. A record that is also on the follow list keeps only its games and its random code. Its alert code and alert settings are deleted, and its games are removed within 90 days if you stop using the app. If the app is still installed, it sets itself up again the next time you open it and alerts carry on. A follow-list record is reused. A device with alerts only gets a new record with a new random code. You may miss alerts sent in between.

How the clean up works. To find the right record, our checking holds a scrambled copy of the alert code for a few minutes. It is deleted before anything is published, and our logs hold counts only.

You can also email support@codeconnectors.co and we will remove your record.

What we never send

Your Apple ID, your device's name or model, advertising ids, your location, what you read, tap or search, your Owned or Wishlist marks, photos, contacts, or any feedback you write. TestFlight testers' email addresses stay with Apple and never reach our server.

Everything else (your library, reading position, settings) stays on your device.
